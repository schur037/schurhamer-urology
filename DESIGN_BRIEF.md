# Design Brief — Dr. Benjamin Schurhamer Urology

## Brand positioning
**Personal expert, world-class training, rooted in the Ozarks.** Dr. Schurhamer is the
face and the relationship. His **University of Pennsylvania (Penn) training** is the
credibility anchor — "Ivy-League pedigree, here at home." **The Ozarks / Northwest
Arkansas** gives a sense of place and warmth. Advanced/robotic surgery is the
differentiator that says "this isn't ordinary urology care."

One-line essence: *Precision urologic oncology — Penn-trained expertise, advanced
technology, rooted in the Ozarks.*

> Brand priority order: **1) Dr. Schurhamer → 2) Penn-trained pedigree →
> 3) Ozarks roots → 4) robotic/advanced surgery.**

> ⚠️ Do **not** use Washington Regional branding. For Penn, reflect the *pedigree*
> ("Trained at the University of Pennsylvania") rather than reproducing Penn's logo or
> trademarked marks — credentials, not their brand assets.

## 1. Color system
| Role | Color | Use |
|------|-------|-----|
| Primary | Navy `#1a365d` | Headers, nav, primary buttons, headings |
| Secondary | Teal `#2c7a7b` | Links, secondary accents, section dividers |
| **Tech accent** | Bright cyan `#38b2ac` / `#0bc5ea` | **Robotic-surgery callouts ONLY** — keep it rare so it reads as "advanced" |
| **Ozarks warm accent** | Warm stone / clay `#b08968` or forest `#386641` | Place/warmth touches — section backgrounds, "roots" motifs, photography framing |
| Neutrals | `#f7fafc` / `#718096` / `#e2e8f0` | Backgrounds, body text, borders |

Keep navy + teal as the spine. Add **two disciplined accents**: a bright **tech** color
reserved for advanced-surgery content, and a warm **Ozarks** earth tone for the
sense-of-place and human-warmth moments. Used sparingly, each stays meaningful.

## 2. Brand hierarchy (Dr. Schurhamer first)
- **Logo lockup:** "Benjamin Schurhamer, MD" as the primary mark, top-left of the sticky nav.
- **Pedigree, supporting:** a small credential line — *"University of Pennsylvania–trained"*
  — near the hero and in the About bio. Communicates training, not a co-brand.
- **Place, supporting:** *"Serving the Ozarks / Northwest Arkansas"* in the footer, contact
  area, and hero subline. Grounds the practice geographically.
- All three affiliations stay **smaller and muted** — never competing with his name.

## 3. Typography
- **Headings:** a refined serif (*Newsreader* or *Source Serif 4*) for physician-researcher
  gravitas — reinforces the Penn-trained, scholarly tone.
- **Body + UI:** keep **Inter** — clean, modern, readable.
- Serif/sans pairing = "trusted expert" (serif) + "modern tech practice" (sans).

## 4. Signature components
1. **Hero** — full-width, navy base, with *subtle* line-art suggesting precision (fine grid
   lines or a stylized instrument silhouette — never a literal stock-photo robot).
   Headline leads with the personal/clinical promise; small sublines carry "Penn-trained"
   and "rooted in the Ozarks."
2. **"Why robotic surgery" feature band** — 3 icons (smaller incisions · faster recovery ·
   greater precision) in the tech-accent color.
3. **Outcomes counters** — animated count-up stats from your dashboard data ("X robotic
   cases," length-of-stay, margin rates). Strongest "advanced practice" proof.
4. **Ozarks sense-of-place band** — a warm section (clay/forest accent) with a rolling-hills
   line motif and local/regional language. Balances the high-tech feel with warmth and roots.
5. **Pedigree / trust row** — board certifications, "University of Pennsylvania–trained,"
   technology partners.
6. **Warm CTA** — "Schedule a Consultation," prominent, so the high-tech feel stays human.

## 5. Tone guardrails
- High-tech but **warm** — advanced technology + Ozarks roots, never cold sci-fi.
- Tech-accent and Ozarks-accent colors used sparingly = each stays special.
- Penn reflected as *training pedigree*, not a borrowed brand.
- Personal photography of Dr. Schurhamer and the region > generic medical stock.
