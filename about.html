About

Benjamin Schurhamer, MD

Urologic Oncologist · Washington Regional – Ozark Urology, Fayetteville, AR

Practice: Washington Regional – Ozark Urology, Fayetteville, AR

Focus: Urologic oncology · prostate cancer · focal therapy · minimally invasive urology

Board Certification: American Board of Urology (2024)

Fellowship: Endourology / Minimally Invasive Oncology, University of Pennsylvania

I am a fellowship-trained urologic oncologist at Washington Regional – Ozark Urology in Fayetteville, Arkansas, bringing Ivy League surgical training from the University of Pennsylvania to comprehensive urologic care. My practice spans the full range of urologic cancers — prostate, bladder, and kidney — as well as benign conditions such as enlarged prostate, kidney stones, and overactive bladder. For prostate cancer in particular, I emphasize advanced imaging (mpMRI and PSMA PET), genomic classifiers such as Decipher, and focal therapies including high-intensity focused ultrasound (HIFU), irreversible electroporation (IRE), and cryoablation. My goal is to deliver precise, risk-adapted, and patient-centered care that balances oncologic control with preservation of urinary and sexual function.

Academically, I lead and collaborate on translational and clinical research exploring imaging–genomic correlations, immune signatures, and molecular predictors of outcomes in prostate cancer. I am committed to generating rigorous, practice-changing data that advances precision oncology and informs real-world clinical decision-making. I also train residents and fellows in oncologic surgery, robotic techniques, and the critical appraisal of evidence.

Beyond clinical and research work, I have helped lead a Summer Internship in Religion and Spirituality, guiding students in exploring the intersection of faith, medicine, and professional identity — integrating ethical reflection, service, and compassion into the care of the whole person.
