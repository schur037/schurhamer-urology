Overview

I am a fellowship-trained urologic oncologist bringing Ivy League surgical training — completed at the University of Pennsylvania — to comprehensive urologic care in Northwest Arkansas. My practice spans the full range of urologic cancers — prostate, bladder, and kidney — alongside the benign conditions that affect everyday quality of life, from enlarged prostate and kidney stones to overactive bladder.

For prostate cancer, I bring particular expertise in advanced imaging (mpMRI and PSMA PET), genomic classifiers such as Decipher, and minimally invasive and focal therapies including HIFU, irreversible electroporation (IRE), and cryoablation. My goal is simple: precise, risk-adapted, patient-centered care that delivers the latest evidence and techniques to every patient — and I’m excited to bring this training back home to Arkansas.
