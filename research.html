Research

Active Studies & Projects

My research program focuses on translating advances in imaging, genomics, and focal therapy into more precise, equitable prostate cancer care.

Active

The Penn Cohort — Imaging & Genomic Risk Stratification

A retrospective cohort integrating mpMRI, genomic classifiers (Decipher), and pathology to refine prostate cancer risk prediction — including whether MRI lesions predict genomic risk and the role of imaging and genomics in African American men.

Collaborators: Alkhatib, Lee, Bivalacqua, Pierorazio, Lloyd-Harris (Penn Urology)

Active

Focal Therapy for Localized & Salvage Prostate Cancer

Evaluating focal ablative therapies — including pulsed electric field (irreversible electroporation), HIFU, and cryoablation — for primary and salvage treatment of localized prostate cancer, with attention to oncologic control and functional preservation.

Collaborators: Lattanzio, Alkhatib, Guzzo, Lee, Pierorazio, Bivalacqua

Published

Health Equity in Prostate Cancer Screening

National analyses of prostate cancer screening patterns and access across populations — including LGBTQ+ patients, smokers, and by race and ethnicity — using large behavioral and cancer databases.

Collaborators: Harmatz, Alkhatib, Roberson, Leff, Guzzo, Pierorazio

Published

Endourology Outcomes & the Cost of Stone Surgery

Work with the Endourological Society TOWER Research Collaborative examining the social and financial impact of stone surgery, alongside machine-learning approaches to a "urinary tract atlas" and stone-passage prediction.

Collaborators: Ziemba, Michel, Fischer, Tasian (Penn Urology)

Innovation

Revolutionizing Pre-Visit Education in Urology

A novel approach to patient education using EPIC MyChart Care Companion to prepare patients before their urology visits — presented at Epic UGM 2025.
